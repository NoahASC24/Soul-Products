How it's made

Process

Every model follows the same path — from a default cube to something you can explore or play.

01

Start with a cube

Block out the big shapes and proportions with simple primitives before any detail.

02

Shape & detail

Refine the mesh — bevels, loops and modifiers until the silhouette reads well.

03

Material & light

Add colour, textures and lighting to give the scene its mood and atmosphere.

04

Bring it to life

Render a demo, or import it into Roblox Studio as a game-ready asset.

About

It all started
with a cube…

Originally, I thought Blender was a complex piece of software that only professionals used. Eventually my models — built from basic Roblox parts — weren't looking the way I wanted without outside help.

My Blender journey started out ambitious, with my first ever person model. Or… something like one. I got better over time, and now I use my Blender models as assets for the games I make.

It's fun to watch a simple cube transform into something huge.
